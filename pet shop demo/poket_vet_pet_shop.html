Frequently Asked Questions for Pet Owners

How do I schedule a veterinary checkup or grooming session?

Navigate to the Services or Veterinary tab on the top menu, select your preferred service, and click "Book Now" to reserve a time slot through your basket.

What pet foods are best for sensitive stomachs?

We offer specialized nutrition like Royal Canin and Me-O adult formulated feeds. Consult our vet team via the Admin or Consultation panel for specific recommendations.

Are all animals listed health-certified?

Yes, all pets and breeds available under breeding or adopt/shop categories are fully vaccinated, dewormed, and health-checked by certified veterinarians.
